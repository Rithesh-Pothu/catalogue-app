# 05: Product page

**What to build:** A visitor clicks a product tile anywhere and sees that product's page with image, title, price in US dollars, description, category and an Enquire button. A non-featured product also shows its rating when it has one. A featured product shows no rating. An unknown product shows a not-found page.

**Blocked by:** 02: Home page shows the featured products.
