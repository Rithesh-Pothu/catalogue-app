# Product catalogue

The language for browsing products and leaving an enquiry.

## Language

**Product**:
A single item in the catalogue. It belongs to exactly one category.
_Avoid_: Item, SKU, listing

**Category**:
A named group of products.
_Avoid_: Department, collection, tag

**Featured product**:
A product the business has chosen to highlight on the home page and at the top of its category.
_Avoid_: Top rated, bestseller, promoted

**Enquiry**:
One request from an enquirer about one product, made of their name, email address and a message.
_Avoid_: Lead, order, inquiry, contact request

**Sales inbox**:
The single mailbox that receives every enquiry.
_Avoid_: Admin email, support inbox

**Enquirer**:
A person who asks about a product by leaving contact details. An enquirer has no account.
_Avoid_: Customer, user, account, buyer
